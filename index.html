Questions

Does it work on every phone?

Yes. It opens in the phone's browser from a WhatsApp link, on Android and iPhone. Guests don't install anything or sign up.

Will older relatives manage it?

That is who we design for first. Large text, one tap to make it larger still, Hindi with one tap, and nothing to log into.

Our family does some rites differently. What then?

Families differ, and that's expected. Your purohit reviews the order before the link goes out, and you can leave out or reorder any rite.

Can we change things after sending the link?

Yes. Times, venues and names can change at any point, and guests always see the latest version through the same link.

Can guests see who else is coming?

No. Replies go only to the family.

How long does it stay online?

A year after the wedding. You also get a keepsake of all the blessings and voice notes to keep for good.

Our wedding is outside India. Does that matter?

Not at all. Guests can open it anywhere, and you can add directions, travel notes and time zones.

Is there a language other than English and Hindi?

For Kashmiri Pandit weddings, English and Hindi for now. Each new tradition comes in its own language too.
