The wedding at a glance

Good to know

The evening runs late

The Lagan begins when the baraat arrives around 5:30 PM and Posh Pooza usually comes late in the night. Jammu evenings in February are cold, so bring a warm shawl.

Dinner

A traditional Kashmiri Pandit vegetarian spread after Posh Pooza: dum oluv, nadir yakhin, chaman qaliya, haakh and rice, with kahwa to finish.

Taking part

You are welcome to sit close. During Posh Pooza every guest is invited to shower the couple with flowers; petals are handed out.

From Livun to Phirlath

Kashmiri Pandit weddings unfold over several days. Here is what happens when, and which gatherings are open to all guests.

It began with Kasamdry, the formal engagement, when the elders of both families met at a temple, exchanged flowers and sweets, and the Tickoos hosted a meal. Around the Maenziraat, the Razdans send Snazaroo: a hamper of bridal adornments for Aditi to wear on the wedding morning.
