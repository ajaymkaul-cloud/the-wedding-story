From Livun to Phirlath

Kashmiri Pandit weddings unfold over several days. Here is what happens when, and which gatherings are open to all guests.

It began with Kasamdry, when the elders of both families met at a temple and exchanged flowers and good wishes. Before the wedding the Razdans send Snazaroo to Aditi, gifts that traditionally include a mirror, sindoor and a betel leaf wrapped in gold foil.

    Good to know

    The evening runs late

    The Lagan begins when the baraat arrives around 5:30 PM and Posh Puza usually comes late in the night. Jammu evenings in February are cold, so bring a warm shawl.

    Dinner

    A traditional Kashmiri Pandit vegetarian spread after Posh Puza: dum oluv, nadir yakhin, chaman qaliya, haakh and rice, with kahwa to finish.

    Taking part

    You are welcome to sit close. During Posh Puza every guest is invited to shower the couple with flowers; petals are handed out.
